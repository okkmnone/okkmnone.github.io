The Maison

Crafted in motion,
finished by hand.

Every Aurelion calibre is assembled twice — once to prove it runs, once to perfect it. Anglage is drawn by hand along every bridge; each screw is thermally blued; each wheel is circled until it sings at four hertz.

Open-heart architecture

The going train is laid bare beneath a domed sapphire. Barrel, centre wheel, escapement and balance are staged in depth so light travels through the movement.

Hand-finished finishing

Côtes de Genève swept across the plates, mirrors polished on interior angles, engravings filled by a single lacquer artist in our Vallée de Joux atelier.

Chronometer certified

Regulated in five positions over fifteen days to −2/+4 seconds per day, then cycled from −5 °C to +50 °C before it earns the caseback signature.
